FAQ

Common
questions

How do sessions run?
All sessions are conducted remotely over video call, with materials prepared and shared in advance. Sessions are 60 minutes and can be weekly or biweekly depending on your student's needs. Every session is tailored to what your student is struggling with, their current coursework, and exam prep — closer to the AP exam, we shift focus to exam preparation.
Do you offer a trial session?
Yes — every new client starts with a free 20-minute introductory call to discuss your student's goals, current level, and whether we're a good fit before committing to a program.
What's the free diagnostic session?
Alongside the introductory call, I offer a free diagnostic analysis: send me your student's most recent AP Biology test, or let me know they're willing to answer 10 AP Biology questions I'll send over, and I'll review it and send you a short written assessment identifying whether the gap is content knowledge or FRQ (free-response) technique — so you know exactly where your student stands before committing to anything.
What grade levels do you work with?
Primarily rising juniors and seniors at US private schools taking or preparing for AP Biology, as well as students developing independent research for college applications.
