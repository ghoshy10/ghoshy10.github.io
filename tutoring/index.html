FAQ

Common
questions

How do sessions run?
All sessions are conducted remotely over video call, with materials prepared and shared in advance. Session length and frequency depend on the programme — see the three options above.
What time zones do you work with?
I'm based in Cambridge, UK, and regularly work with students across EST, CST, and PST, with morning and evening slots available to fit US school schedules.
Do you offer a trial session?
Yes — every new family starts with a free 20-minute introductory call to discuss the student's goals, current level, and whether we're a good fit before committing to a programme.
What grade levels do you work with?
Primarily rising juniors and seniors at US private schools taking or preparing for AP Biology, as well as students developing independent research for college applications.
When should we get in touch?
Summer bootcamp spots are limited and fill up before July–August. Academic-year weekly prep can start any time from September onwards. Reach out early to secure a slot.
